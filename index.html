RExtract

Welcome to RExtract, the data extraction tool for Flashback on the Atari ST (REminiscence).

Drop your four Amiga Flashback installation disk images below to extract the data, give everything ST-friendly 8.3 filenames and put it all in a DATA folder you can copy next to FLASHBAK.TOS; it does not currently extract and convert the music files.

Everything happens in this browser. Your disk images are never uploaded.

Drop your four disk images here

Disk images must be .ipf, .adf or .adz format

    IPF decoding is derived from MAME's ipf_dsk.cpp by Olivier Galibert, BSD-3-Clause.

    Flashback for Atari ST is available from neilrackett.com/atarist
