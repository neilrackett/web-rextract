RExtract

Welcome to RExtract, the data extraction tool for Flashback on the Atari ST (REminiscence).

Drop your four Amiga installation disk images below to extract everything you need to play Flashback on your Atari ST and put it all into DATA and MUSIC folders that you can copy next to FLASHBAK.TOS on your hard disk.

Music is automatically converted to YM tracks that can be played on any ST, but it's still experimental so you'll need to enable it by adding music=true to RS.CFG.

Everything happens in this browser. Your disk images are never uploaded.

Drop your four disk images here

Disk images must be .ipf, .adf or .adz format
